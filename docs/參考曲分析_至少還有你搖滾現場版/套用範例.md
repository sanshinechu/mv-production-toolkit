# 搖滾現場版套用範例

把 [README.md](README.md) 分析出來的配方（頭中尾歡呼、爬樓梯、高潮前先收）實際套到兩首歌上。

> **緣起**：原本想做周杰倫《白色風車》，2026-09-11 **Suno 偵測到版權歌詞直接擋下**。
> 不走改錯字、同音字這類繞法（違反 Suno 條款、帳號可能被停，歌詞也仍是別人的），
> 改成下面兩首：歌詞都是自己的，Suno 不會擋，要上傳 YouTube 也沒有版權問題。

## 一、原創新歌《陪你淋雨》（男聲 power ballad）

溫柔浪漫、「雨不停我也不走」的感覺，場景放在宜蘭（羅東街角、冬山河）。全新創作，**不是改寫《白色風車》**。

### Style

> Live arena power ballad, Mandopop love song, around 72 BPM with a half-time rock feel, warm male lead vocal that is soft and intimate in the verses and grows into passionate, soaring belted choruses, opening with gentle piano, clean electric guitar and soft rain ambience under light crowd cheering, gradual build-up with each chorus fuller than the last, melodic emotional guitar solo, dramatic band stop with only vocal and crowd before the final chorus, explosive full-band last chorus with backing vocals, gentle piano-and-vocal ending, big sustained final chord and crowd applause.

> Live arena power ballad, Mandopop, warm male vocal, soft piano and rain intro, crowd cheering, gradual build-up, guitar solo, band stop, explosive final chorus, gentle ending with applause

## 二、畢業歌 搖滾現場版（女聲）

原曲：[畢業影片MV製作對話記錄_20260429.md](../../畢業影片MV製作對話記錄_20260429.md) 的 Lo-Fi 治癒版。
**歌詞一字未改**，只換結構標籤、加一段吉他獨奏和齊奏重拍，Bridge 改成樂團急停。
想像成畢業典禮上全校一起唱的現場。

### Style

> Live rock concert version of a heartfelt Mandopop graduation song, around 120 BPM, bright and emotional female lead vocal that starts gentle and nostalgic and builds to powerful, uplifting choruses, crowd of students cheering at the start and singing along on the final chorus, clean electric guitar intro without drums, then overdriven guitars, driving live drums and warm bass, gradual build-up with each chorus bigger than the last, melodic guitar solo, dramatic band stop on the bridge with only vocal and crowd, band hits in unison, explosive full-band last chorus, big sustained ending with applause.

精簡版：

> Live rock concert, Mandopop graduation song, 120 BPM, emotional female vocal, crowd cheering and singing along, guitar solo, band stop, explosive final chorus, applause ending

### Lyrics

## 生成與挑選

- 每首同設定**生 4～6 首**再挑
- 先聽**急停那段**（新歌是 `[Break]`、畢業歌是 `[Bridge]`）：樂團有沒有真的停、只剩人聲，是成敗關鍵
- 挑好的成品可以用 `分析腳本/` 跑一次，跟 README 第四節的「劇本」對照能量有沒有逐段往上爬
